How to Activate Hulu

  1. Open Hulu app on your device.
  2. Get activation code on screen.
  3. Go to Hulu.com/activate in browser.
  4. Login with your Hulu account.
  5. Enter activation code.
  6. Click Activate and start streaming.

FAQ

Why use Hulu.com/activate?

It connects your account to your TV or streaming device securely.

Why is my code not working?

Codes expire quickly. Restart the app to generate a new one.

Can I use multiple devices?

Yes, Hulu supports multiple devices with one account.
